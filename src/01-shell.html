Drop an OBD-II datalog to analyse it

CSV or tab-delimited text from Torque, OBDLink, Car Scanner, ScanXL and similar loggers. Every chart and finding is computed right here in this browser.

Reads
Comma, semicolon or tab delimited, with or without metadata header lines
Maps
~20 OBD channels by name, with a manual override for anything it misses
Converts
°F to °C, mph to km/h, AFR to lambda, feet to metres
Diagnoses
Fuel trim, bank balance, load dependence, thermals, catalyst, charging, log quality

Every log you open here is also saved to this server's database for history and duplicate detection — re-opening the same export is recognised, not re-saved. Satellite map tiles are requested only if a log carries GPS and only when the satellite basemap is on.
